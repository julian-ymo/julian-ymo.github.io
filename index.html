NO. 05 — QUESTIONS

Questions & answers.

Is this the same app as Film Desk?

Yes. Film Desk is now called Lumora Film, with a new name and logo. The new version installs over the old one, so your recipes and settings stay.

Is Lumora Film free?

Yes. Lumora Film is free to download and use. In a future update, it will show some ads to help keep it free.

Can I edit RAW files?

Yes, in the Android app and the web version: RAF, DNG, CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW, ORF, RW2 and more. In the app, use Files on the Import screen if the photo picker doesn't show them.

How do I install the APK?

Tap "Download for Android". It opens the file on TeraBox: download the APK, open it, and allow installs when Android asks. New versions install over the old one.

How do I install it from my browser?

Open the web version, then add it like an app. It gets its own icon, and once it's installed you can use it offline, with no internet connection.

  • PC or Mac (Chrome, Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install.
  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos are edited on your own device and never leave it.

Is it made by a camera company?

No. Lumora Film is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any camera maker.
