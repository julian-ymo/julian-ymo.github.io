Questions

Is Film Desk free?

Yes. Film Desk is free to download and use.

How do I install the APK?

Tap "Download for Android" on your phone. It opens the file on TeraBox: download FilmDesk.apk from there, open it, and allow installs when Android asks. New versions install over the old one, so your app stays in place.

Can I edit RAW files?

Yes, in the Android app and the web version. Import RAF, DNG, CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW, ORF, RW2 and other RAW files. In the app, use Files on the Import screen if the photo picker doesn't show them. Opening a RAW takes a few seconds.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos are edited on your own device and never leave it.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. Open the web version in any modern browser on a PC, Mac or tablet. Nothing to install. It edits JPEG, PNG and RAW files.

Is Film Desk made by a camera company?

No. Film Desk is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any camera maker.
